Forecast and place data from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0) — free, no API key. Daily model coverage runs today −5 through today +15. A date one or two days past the edge borrows the nearest covered day; anything further is answered instead by a ten-year climate normal — the mean of every observed day within ±3 days of that calendar date across the last ten years, from the ERA5 reanalysis archive. Normals describe what the place is usually like, not what the weather will do; they are marked with a blue rail and never counted as a forecast. Temperatures are fetched in metric and converted in the page, so switching units costs no extra requests. Your itinerary is saved in this browser only — each device and browser keeps its own, and clearing site data loses it, so use Export to keep a copy you can reload here or carry to another device.
